Reputable Cooking & Recipe Websites

A hand-picked list of cooking sites worth bookmarking — from rigorously tested recipe libraries to independent food blogs and regional cuisine specialists.

Featured

  • Skip To Recipes
    Cuts the long-winded story and gets you straight to the ingredients and instructions.

General Recipe Collections

  • Serious Eats
    Tested recipes with the science behind why they work.
  • BBC Good Food
    Huge library of reliable, well-rated recipes.
  • Bon Appétit
    Recipes and food writing from the magazine of the same name.
  • Food Network
    Recipes from well-known chefs and TV shows.
  • Allrecipes
    Community-driven recipes with thousands of user reviews.
  • Epicurious
    Recipes from Bon Appétit, Gourmet, and other established sources.
  • NYT Cooking
    Editor-curated recipes from the New York Times.

Technique & Learning

  • King Arthur Baking
    Authoritative resource for baking recipes and technique.
  • America's Test Kitchen
    Rigorously tested recipes and equipment reviews.
  • Cook's Illustrated
    Deep dives into the why behind recipes.

Independent Food Blogs

  • Smitten Kitchen
    Long-running home cooking blog by Deb Perelman.
  • Budget Bytes
    Cost-conscious recipes with per-serving price breakdowns.
  • Half Baked Harvest
    Approachable seasonal recipes.
  • Minimalist Baker
    Recipes requiring 10 ingredients or fewer.
  • The Kitchn
    Recipes, kitchen tips, and reviews.

Regional & Cuisine-Specific

  • Just One Cookbook
    Authentic Japanese home cooking.
  • The Woks of Life
    Chinese family recipes spanning regional styles.
  • Maangchi
    Korean cooking from a beloved video creator.
  • Saveur
    Recipes and stories from cuisines around the world.
