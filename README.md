# Recipe List

A curated list of reputable cooking and recipe websites worth bookmarking.

## Featured

- [Skip To Recipes](https://www.skipto.recipes) — Cuts the long-winded story and gets you straight to the ingredients and instructions.

## General Recipe Collections

- [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com) — Tested recipes with the science behind why they work.
- [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com) — Huge library of reliable, well-rated recipes.
- [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com) — Recipes and food writing from the magazine of the same name.
- [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com) — Recipes from well-known chefs and TV shows.
- [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com) — Community-driven recipes with thousands of user reviews.
- [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com) — Recipes from Bon Appétit, Gourmet, and other established sources.
- [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com) — Editor-curated recipes from the New York Times.

## Technique & Learning

- [King Arthur Baking](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com) — Authoritative resource for baking recipes and technique.
- [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com) — Rigorously tested recipes and equipment reviews.
- [Cook's Illustrated](https://www.cooksillustrated.com) — Deep dives into the why behind recipes.

## Independent Food Blogs

- [Smitten Kitchen](https://smittenkitchen.com) — Long-running home cooking blog by Deb Perelman.
- [Budget Bytes](https://www.budgetbytes.com) — Cost-conscious recipes with per-serving price breakdowns.
- [Half Baked Harvest](https://www.halfbakedharvest.com) — Approachable seasonal recipes.
- [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com) — Recipes requiring 10 ingredients or fewer.
- [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com) — Recipes, kitchen tips, and reviews.

## Regional & Cuisine-Specific

- [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com) — Authentic Japanese home cooking.
- [The Woks of Life](https://thewoksoflife.com) — Chinese family recipes spanning regional styles.
- [Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com) — Korean cooking from a beloved video creator.
- [Saveur](https://www.saveur.com) — Recipes and stories from cuisines around the world.
